Every entry is a question the two bodies kept coming back to. Each one carries a dated chronology quoted from English Wikipedia, and the corpus attaches it to every meeting whose own agenda — or whose adopted resolution's title — names that subject. A meeting only ever receives the part of the chronology that had already happened when it sat.

每个专题条目都是两个机构反复讨论的一个问题。每个条目附有一份引自英文维基百科的带日期大事记；凡会议议程——或会上通过的决议标题——提及该主题，语料库就会把条目挂到这次会议上。每次会议只会得到在它召开之前已经发生的那部分大事记。

An entry's size measures the corpus, not the conflict. Syria carries six meetings and Russia–Ukraine eleven, not because those wars were quiet at the UN but because the Council files them under generic agenda headings that are deliberately not matched, and because a vetoed draft never gets a resolution number to be indexed by. Where an entry has a known limit of this kind, its own page says so.
条目的大小衡量的是语料库，而不是冲突本身。 叙利亚只有 6 次会议、俄乌只有 11 次，并不是因为这两场战争在联合国很安静，而是因为安理会把它们归在通用的议程标题之下，而这类标题被刻意排除在匹配之外；同时，被否决的草案永远拿不到决议编号，也就无从索引。凡条目存在这类已知局限，其页面都会注明。
