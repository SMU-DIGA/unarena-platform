本页后半部分基本上只关于安理会。 大会的 JSON 只记录每次会议的主席国——没有成员或受邀名单——且只有 929 条国家层面的表决记录。因此地图和表格几乎完全由安理会数据构成（成员出席 次、受邀 次、担任主席 次）。把本页当作大会的写照是错误的。
This second half of the page is essentially about the Security Council. The Assembly JSON records only the presiding country for each meeting — no member or invited lists — and holds just 929 country-level vote records. The map and the table are therefore built almost entirely from Council data ( member appearances, invitations, presidencies). Reading this page as a portrait of the Assembly would be wrong.

Shading is a continuous magnitude on a single-hue ramp, light to dark. Grey means no record under that metric. Historical entities — the USSR, Yugoslavia, Zaire — keep their own identity in the table but borrow their successor's polygon to appear on the map at all; that is a compromise for drawing, not a claim that the two are the same. Six states (Maldives, Monaco, San Marino, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Nauru, Marshall Islands) have no polygon in the basemap, so they appear in the table but cannot be shaded.

The corpus contains 439 distinct country strings, 209 of which occur three times or fewer and are mostly OCR noise (Aus t r a l i a, Fran~e). After normalisation of country records resolve; the remaining are unresolvable or are not states at all (the PLO, the Secretary-General) and are excluded.
