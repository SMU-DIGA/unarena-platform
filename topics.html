These labels are keyword hits, not an adjudicated subject assignment. The geo and func labels come from keyword matching against resolution text, and sampling found mislabels — S/RES/2402 concerns Yemen but is tagged iraq; S/RES/1467 falls into the Africa bucket on the strength of the single word africa. Read this page as how often a category's keywords appear in the text, not as the true share of the agenda. One resolution can match several categories, so the shares sum to more than 100%.
