1946 — 2025 · Security Council & General Assembly

Eighty years of UN voting,
as the records tell it.

The UN's two voting chambers have never kept the same beat. In the years the veto had the Council seized up, the Assembly was busy inventing a way around it; in the 1990s, when Council output hit its all-time peak, the Assembly corpus happens to have its largest hole. This page puts both on one calendar-year axis and walks the eighty years through 29 milestones and 32 topic entries.

Every node is pinned to real records — open one for that year's tally, which states voted against, and whether the record itself can be trusted.
