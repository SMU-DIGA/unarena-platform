Activity timeline, 1946—2025

Annual output from both bodies, aligned on the calendar year. The Assembly corpus is stored by session (the Nth session opens in September and runs into the following September), so each resolution has been re-dated here using the adoption date printed in its own text — which is what makes a year-for-year comparison possible. Drag the slider below to zoom any stretch; all three panels move together.

Annual output and voting structure

Three panels share one time axis and one zoom slider. No dual y-axis is used — two series of different magnitude laid over one another cross wherever the axis scaling puts them rather than where the data does, and that artefact is deliberately excluded from this project.

1980 至 1992 年间的大会数据不能解读。 这一时段有 7 届会议的语料库覆盖率仅为 13.4%–44%（1991 年最低，为 13.4%），另有 5 届（1980–82、1984、1986）在 59.9% 到 79.9% 之间。它们以斜线填充的空心柱显示。这些低谷是收集缺口，并不代表大会实际产出下降，更不应被解读为冷战后期的某种政治现象。
1964 年是唯一的例外。当年覆盖率为 100%，但大会只通过了 2 项决议。围绕《宪章》第十九条（拖欠会费的国家丧失表决权）的争执，使第 19 届会议根本无法表决，会议推迟到 1964 年 12 月 30 日才通过任何决议。这是整个时段中唯一一个语料库完整而产出确实崩塌的年份，因此也是唯一可以作为结论的低谷。
The Assembly line cannot be read between 1980 and 1992. Seven sessions in that stretch sit at 13.4%–44% corpus coverage (1991 is the worst, at 13.4%), with a further five (1980–82, 1984, 1986) between 59.9% and 79.9%. They are drawn as hatched, hollow bars. These troughs are collection gaps, not a decline in the Assembly's real output, and they should certainly not be read as a political phenomenon of the late Cold War.
1964 is the one exception. Coverage that year is 100%, yet the Assembly adopted just 2 resolutions. The fight over Article 19 of the Charter — the loss of voting rights for states in arrears — kept the 19th session from voting at all, and the session was pushed back to 30 December 1964 before it adopted anything. It is the only year in the whole span where the corpus is complete and output genuinely collapsed, so it is the only trough that can be treated as a finding.
